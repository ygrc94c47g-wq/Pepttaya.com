FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I order?

Message us on WhatsApp. We'll send you our current product list, you reply with the items and quantities you need, and we'll confirm availability and send your quote with payment instructions.

Where do you deliver, and how fast?

We deliver across Thailand, and we aim to get orders out quickly. Once your order is dispatched, we send you the tracking details on WhatsApp.

Can I see lab results?

Yes. Our products are third-party lab tested, and Certificates of Analysis are available on request. Ask for the CoAs on WhatsApp.

Can I order several items at once?

Yes. Just list everything you need, with quantities, in one WhatsApp message and we'll put it all on a single quote.

How should products be stored?

Keep products sealed in their original packaging, in a cool, dry place away from direct light, and follow any storage directions on the label.

Are these products for human use?

No. All products are sold strictly for laboratory research use only and are not for human or veterinary use. Buyers are responsible for complying with the laws that apply where they are.
